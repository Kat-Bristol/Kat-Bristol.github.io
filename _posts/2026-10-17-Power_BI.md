A Power BI dashboard project, using official Department for Education (DfE) data to explore how A-Level Results and subject participation vary across academic years, subjects and student characteristics. The dashboard enables users to explore attainment trends, compare student numbers across selected subjects and examine the distribution of grades.


---

Key Questions

How have A-level attainment rates changed over these five academic years?

How do results differ by subject and student group?

Which subjects attract the most students within selected groups?

How should changes in grading arrangements after COVID-19 affect our interpretation of the results?

--- 

Data
Source: Department for Education — Explore Education Statistics
Dataset: Student counts and Results - A level by subject and student characteristics (end of 16-18 study)
Coverage: England, academic years 2020/21–2024/25.



Dashboard Features

Attainment overview: KPI cards display total students, A* attainment, A*–C attainment and year-on-year changes.

Five-year attainment trend: A line chart shows A*–C attainment across all five academic years, allowing users to assess the longer-term pattern.

Grade distribution: A column chart shows student counts by grade, with custom tooltips displaying counts and percentage shares.

Subject comparison: A bar chart compares student numbers across seven selected subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Maths, Maths (Further), Psychology and Geography.

Subject drillthrough: Users can open a dedicated subject page with a dynamic heading identifying the selected subject.

Interactive filtering: Slicers allow exploration by academic year, subject and student group. A reset button restores a consistent starting view.

---

Methodology: In brief, the dataset was cleaned and transformed in Power Query; then organised into a star-schema model with a central results table and dimensions for year, subject, grade and student breakdown. 

Data preparation: Power Query, data cleaning, data types, handling suppressed values

Data modelling: Star schema, dimension tables, one-to-many relationships

DAX Measures: CALCULATE, DIVIDE, SELECTEDVALUE, FILTER, REMOVEFILTERS

Visualisation: KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, grade distributions

Interactivity: Slicers, drillthrough, custom tooltips, bookmarks, dynamic text

Data storytelling: Trend analysis, comparisons, contextual interpretation



Important analytical considerations

Suppressed and unavailable values were treated as missing rather than zero.

Student counts across overlapping breakdown categories should not be added together indiscriminately.

Grade-share calculations use an appropriate total-students denominator while retaining the selected year, subject and student-group filters.

Year-on-year changes in attainment rates are expressed in percentage points.

Comparisons across pandemic and post-pandemic years require caution because assessment and grading arrangements changed.


---

Key Insights

1. Among the seven selected subjects in 2024/25, Psychology has the highest student count for Female students, while Maths has the highest for Male students. This comparison highlights differences in subject participation but does not establish why those differences exist.

2. The dashboard reveals a generally downward A*–C attainment trend across 2020/21–2024/25 for several selected subjects and student groups. Some explored selections show declines of approximately 10–15 percentage points. However, these results span major changes in how A-level grades were awarded:

2020/21: Teacher-assessed grades replaced examinations.

2021/22: Examinations resumed with transitional support and grading arrangements.

2022/23: Grading returned to pre-pandemic standards.

2023/24–2024/25: Normal grading arrangements continued.

Consequently, the observed decline in recorded attainment should not be interpreted solely as a decline in students' underlying academic performance. The exact magnitude and shape of the trend depend on the selected subject and student group.

How to Explore the Dashboard

Download A_Level_Results_Explorer_Portfolio.pbix and open it in Microsoft Power BI Desktop. Use the academic year, subject and student-group slicers. Hover over grade columns to view detailed tooltips. Right-click a subject in the comparison chart to open its drillthrough page. Use Ctrl+click on the Reset Dashboard button.
